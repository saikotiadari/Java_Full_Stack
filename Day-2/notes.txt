3.Image tag:
----------------
    -> <img> is the one of the media tag used to display images on the web pages.
    ->image  attributes:
    -------------------------
    1.src(source):
        ->absolute path(browser image path)
        ->relative path(downloaded images path)
    2.alt
    3.width
    4.height
    5.style
    6.id
    7.class

attributes:
--------------
        -> attributes are the additional information given to the tag
        -> attributes are always in static (opening tag)
        -> attributes are always in key and value

        ex:<h1 style=" " align="center/right/left"> </h1>
